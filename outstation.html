How outstation pricing works

  • Per-km fare based on the vehicle — hatchback, sedan, SUV or tempo traveller.
  • One-way is charged for your trip's distance only.
  • Round trips are charged for the total distance; a minimum km-per-day may apply, and driver allowance is added for overnight days.
  • Tolls and permits, where applicable, are told to you before you confirm.
