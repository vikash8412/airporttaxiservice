How package cabs work

A package gives you a cab for a fixed block — for example 8 hours / 80 km for local sightseeing, or a longer full-day block for out-of-city tours. Within that block you decide the stops. Go over the hours or kilometres and the extra is charged at a small per-unit rate, told to you up front.

City sightseeing

Half-day and full-day hourly packages for Bangalore's palaces, temples, parks and markets — at your own pace.

Nearby day tours

Nandi Hills, Mysore, Coorg, Chikmagalur, Shivanasamudra and more — leave early, return by night.

What's included

  • Set hours + kilometres — pick the block that fits your plan.
  • Driver included, with driver allowance already in the package price for longer tours.
  • Clear extras — any additional hours or km are charged at a fixed rate you see before booking.
  • Choice of vehicle — hatchback and sedan for couples, SUV or tempo traveller for groups.

Popular starting points

Most sightseeing packages are 8 hours / 80 km within the city. Day tours run longer blocks based on the destination distance. Tell the assistant where you want to go and it will show the right package and fare.
